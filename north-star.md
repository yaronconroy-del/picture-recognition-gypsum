# North Star

This is my final project for a university-level AI course: teaching a model to look at the camera feed on our gypsum dewatering filter and tell, from the picture alone, whether the cake is forming correctly.

The dataset is already collected and sorted — 176 labeled photos across five states (valid/invalid, day/night, plus an empty-filter class). Right now my priority is training an actual model in Python and getting the best recognition accuracy I can.
