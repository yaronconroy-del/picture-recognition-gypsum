Gypsum filter · camera reference

Five states, one camera

The filter camera only ever tells you five things. Here's what each one actually looks like in a real frame, and the one visual cue that gives it away.

By day

Valid תקין יום · day
Valid — Dayתקין יום

The daylight "nothing to see here" shot. One even, ridged blanket of cake runs across every sector with no gaps — the texture looks the same from the near edge to the far edge.

Invalid לא תקין יום · day
Invalid — Dayלא תקין יום

The tell is cracking. Pale fracture lines run across one or more sectors where the cake has pulled apart instead of forming a solid sheet, sometimes with bare patches underneath.

By night

Valid תקין לילה · night
Valid — Nightתקין לילה

Same healthy cake, now lit by the floodlight instead of the sun — expect a hot glare in one corner and warmer tones. Underneath the glare, it's still one continuous surface, no breaks.

Invalid לא תקין לילה · night
Invalid — Nightלא תקין לילה

Harder to catch under floodlight, but the same light-colored fracture lines still cut through the dark cake — look for them near the brightest part of the frame, where contrast is highest.

Either time

Empty מסנן ריק · empty
Empty Filterמסנן ריק

No cake at all — just the bare, smooth filter surface with none of the ridged texture the other four states share. This is usually the filter between cycles, not a bad cake.
